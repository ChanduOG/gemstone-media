The Founder’s
Vision

I started Gemstone Media because I recognized a universal struggle, especially among student founders and student-owned businesses. You may have the drive, the hustle and the product but you lack the exposure and the right network to truly level up. Building a business from the ground up is exhausting when you feel invisible. I created this platform to bridge that gap. While my roots are in supporting student entrepreneurs, our mission is not limited to them. We give all hardworking local founders the spotlight they deserve, assess what they are struggling with and give them the connections they need to thrive.

A bigger platform for local founders to level up.

Gemstone Media is an enterprise initiative providing promotion, resources, and networking support for hard-working entrepreneurs. From emerging student founders to established local brands, we provide the exposure and strategic connections you need to scale your hustle and reach a wider audience.

05 / How it works

From your story to the screen.

Applications are considered individually. Submitting an application does not guarantee selection or a specific commercial result.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Tell us about your business, what you offer, and why you’d like to be featured.

  2. 02

    Plan your story

    If selected, we’ll work with you to shape an interview that brings your business and its value into focus.

  3. 03

    Create the feature

    We film and produce your story with care, aiming for a polished and engaging result.

  4. 04

    Share and connect

    Your feature is published on our platforms, giving potential customers and partners a new way to discover your business.

  5. 05

    Assess & Level Up

    Post-interview, we assess your business needs and connect you with our network of mentors and development partners to help you scale.
