01 / The platform

A bigger platform for South African business.

Gemstone Media is a digital interview platform and production studio built to showcase the people behind South African businesses. We create high-quality video stories that introduce business owners, explain what they offer, and help connect them with a wider audience.

From emerging entrepreneurs to established brands, we believe every business has a story worth sharing.

04 / For business owners

Your business deserves to be seen.

Whether you’re building a new venture or growing an established business, Gemstone Media gives you the opportunity to share what you do with a wider audience. Apply to be interviewed and featured on our platforms, or work with us to create video content for your brand.

A feature can help people understand your business, discover your products or services, and take the next step toward working with you. Results depend on your offer, audience, and how you use the content; we don’t promise specific sales or revenue outcomes.

05 / How it works

From your story to the screen.

Applications are considered individually. Submitting an application does not guarantee selection or a specific commercial result.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Tell us about your business, what you offer, and why you’d like to be featured.

  2. 02

    Plan your story

    If selected, we’ll work with you to shape an interview that brings your business and its value into focus.

  3. 03

    Create the feature

    We film and produce your story with care, aiming for a polished and engaging result.

  4. 04

    Share and connect

    Your feature is published on our platforms, giving potential customers and partners a new way to discover your business.
