# Gemstone Media Policy Setup Checklist

**Status:** Draft for review. Do not publish the policy text as final or use the release draft to obtain consent until these facts are completed and the documents have had appropriate South African legal review.

## Business and privacy operations

- [ ] Confirm the legal name of the person/entity responsible, trading name, physical/business address, and site domain.
- [ ] Confirm the Information Officer’s name/title, registration status, monitored privacy-request address, and process for access, correction, deletion, objection, consent withdrawal, and complaints.
- [ ] Identify the actual web host, server/request logs, access roles, email provider, Google Forms account owner, form respondents/recipients, other processors, and response exports/backups.
- [ ] Confirm Google Forms account region, data location, cross-border transfers, account security/access controls, Google retention settings, and any processors actually used.
- [ ] Set real retention/deletion periods per application, enquiry, project, release, and website log; identify who performs deletion and handles backups.
- [ ] Provide a true, non-sensitive summary of safeguards actually used. Do not promise security outcomes.
- [ ] Review the Google Form’s current questions and response settings before every material change. The inspected form asks for name, business name, email, phone/WhatsApp, location, web/social links (optional), products/services, distinctiveness, help type, goals, and notes (optional).
- [ ] Confirm each required Google Form question is necessary for the chosen enquiry type; remove questions that are not needed.

## Cookies, embeds, and marketing

- [ ] Confirm what cookies, local/session storage, analytics, security tools, and tracking the current Google Forms embed actually activates, and what controls are available before it loads.
- [ ] Confirm whether Google Fonts and server logs are retained/processed, and check for tools added by future hosting/build/deployment settings.
- [ ] If nonessential tracking is present and consent is required, implement a working pre-activation choice and verify acceptance, rejection, withdrawal, and persistence. Do not claim a preference centre works before testing it.
- [ ] Confirm whether Gemstone Media sends any marketing messages outside this website. If so, provide a separate optional unchecked opt-in and working unsubscribe route where required; do not use application data as marketing consent.

## Interview release

- [ ] Confirm which Gemstone-controlled channels/accounts may publish features, the intended edits, formats, term, scope, and permission/licence wording.
- [ ] Define whether interviewees review anything before publication; name the contact and process for concerns, corrections, future-use requests, takedowns, and already redistributed copies.
- [ ] Confirm actual compensation, fees, and expenses, including whether there are none.
- [ ] Create and test a separate signed release capture, return, access, and retention process before recording or publishing. Application submission is not consent.
- [ ] Do not accept or publish people under 18 until a guardian-consent and safeguarding workflow is approved.

## Production terms and website information

- [ ] Complete the production placeholders: quote validity, scope, deliverables, schedule, client duties; deposits, milestones, tax, expenses, late payment; rescheduling, cancellation, refunds; revisions and extra work; delivery; rights to finals, raw footage, project files, music and stock; portfolio use; approvals and releases.
- [ ] Confirm rights holders and permissions for the logo, copy, photographs, video, audio, music, and all third-party materials on the site.
- [ ] If accepting online bookings, orders, or payment later, review the actual flow and required ECTA/CPA supplier and transaction disclosures before launch.
- [ ] Confirm the website and production contact details, then complete all bracketed placeholders in `gemstone/policies.html`.

## Review and release

- [ ] Have South African counsel review the Privacy Notice, Website Terms, Cookie Notice, Interview Release, Production Terms, form wording, and actual Google/hosting configuration.
- [ ] Update the “Last updated” date only when the approved version is published. Remove the draft banner and placeholders only after every item above is resolved.
